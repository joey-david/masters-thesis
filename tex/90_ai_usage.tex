\section*{Use of AI tools}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Use of AI tools}

AI tools assisted with repository search, result-file indexing, literature discovery, code review, structural editing, and prose drafting. Scientific claims in this report were checked against local experiment artifacts or primary-source paper text before inclusion. AI output did not replace the experiment gates, exact evaluators, saved result ledgers, or the author's judgment about which claims the evidence supports.
